# Species catalog

Reference for visual classification. Match what the camera sees against these
descriptions and pick the best-fitting entry.

| species | visual description |
|---|---|
| snake plant (sansevieria trifasciata) | stiff, upright sword-shaped leaves growing straight from the soil, dark green with lighter horizontal banding, often yellow leaf edges; no visible stem |
| golden pothos (epipremnum aureum) | trailing vine with heart-shaped glossy leaves, green with yellow marbling, usually cascading over the pot edge |
| monstera (monstera deliciosa) | very large glossy leaves with deep splits and holes, spreading wide on long stalks |
| ficus (ficus benjamina) | small tree with a woody trunk and many small, pointed, glossy leaves on arching branches |
| peace lily (spathiphyllum) | clump of broad, dark-green lance-shaped leaves; occasional white hood-like flowers on tall stems |
| spider plant (chlorophytum comosum) | narrow arching grass-like leaves, green with a white central stripe, often with dangling plantlets |
| aloe vera | thick, fleshy, triangular leaves with small teeth along the edges, growing in a rosette |
| zz plant (zamioculcas zamiifolia) | upright arching stems lined with pairs of oval, waxy, very dark green leaflets |
| rubber plant (ficus elastica) | thick upright stem with large, oval, leathery leaves, deep green to burgundy, distinctly shiny |
| philodendron (heartleaf) | trailing soft vine with matte, heart-shaped leaves, uniformly green and thinner than pothos |
