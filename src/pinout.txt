================================================
LOGOTS PROJECT: HARDWARE PINOUT
Jetson Orin Nano (40-pin Header)
+ Arduino Uno motor controller + 12V power
================================================

------------------------------------------------
1. INMP441 MICROPHONE (INPUT)
------------------------------------------------
VDD  --> Pin 1 or 17  (Jetson: 3.3V)        *** DO NOT USE 5V ***
GND  --> Pin 39       (Jetson: GND)
L/R  --> Pin 39       (Jetson: GND)          [Sets mic to Mono Left Channel]
SCK  --> Pin 12       (Jetson: i2s2_sclk)    [Shared with Amp BCLK]
WS   --> Pin 35       (Jetson: i2s2_fs)      [Shared with Amp LRC]
SD   --> Pin 38       (Jetson: i2s2_din)

------------------------------------------------
2. MAX98357A AMPLIFIER (OUTPUT)
------------------------------------------------
VIN  --> Pin 2 or 4   (Jetson: 5V)
GND  --> Pin 6        (Jetson: GND)
BCLK --> Pin 12       (Jetson: i2s2_sclk)    [Shared with Mic SCK]
LRC  --> Pin 35       (Jetson: i2s2_fs)      [Shared with Mic WS]
DIN  --> Pin 40       (Jetson: i2s2_dout)

------------------------------------------------
3. GROVE IMU 9DOF V2.2 — MPU-9250 (I2C)
------------------------------------------------
VCC  --> Pin 1        (Jetson: 3.3V)         *** DO NOT USE 5V ***
GND  --> Pin 6        (Jetson: GND)
SDA  --> Pin 3        (Jetson: i2c8)         [Kernel bus: /dev/i2c-7]
SCL  --> Pin 5        (Jetson: i2c8)         [Kernel bus: /dev/i2c-7]

I2C address : 0x68
Detect      : i2cdetect -y -r 7
Orientation : X right, Y forward, Z up (as printed on chip)
Test script : imu_test.py

NOTE: jetson-io labels these pins as i2c8 but the kernel
      exposes the bus as /dev/i2c-7. Always use bus 7.

------------------------------------------------
4. ARDUINO MOTOR CONTROLLER (I2C via Level Shifter)
------------------------------------------------
Board  : Arduino Uno
Shield : HW-130 (L293D) — Adafruit Motor Shield v1 clone (AFMotor.h)

*** LEVEL SHIFTER REQUIRED (e.g. BSS138 4-channel bidirectional) ***
Jetson runs at 3.3V logic; Arduino runs at 5V logic.

  LEVEL SHIFTER — JETSON SIDE (LV = 3.3V):
  LV (Vcc)  --> Pin 1  (Jetson: 3.3V)
  GND       --> Pin 6  (Jetson: GND)
  LV1 / A1  --> Pin 27 (Jetson: i2c1 SDA)   [Kernel bus: /dev/i2c-1]
  LV2 / A2  --> Pin 28 (Jetson: i2c1 SCL)   [Kernel bus: /dev/i2c-1]

  LEVEL SHIFTER — ARDUINO SIDE (HV = 5V):
  HV (Vcc)  --> Arduino 5V
  GND       --> Arduino GND
  HV1 / B1  --> Arduino A4  (SDA)
  HV2 / B2  --> Arduino A5  (SCL)

Arduino I2C address : 0x08
Detect              : i2cdetect -y -r 1   (should show 08)
Firmware            : src/firmware/logots_motor_control/logots_motor_control.ino
Protocol            : "{left_pwm},{right_pwm},{pan_angle},{tilt_angle}\n"

  ARDUINO — MOTOR SHIELD:
  Right motor  : M1 terminal             (see section 5)
  Left motor   : M4 terminal             (see section 5)
  Motor power  : EXT_PWR, 12V            (see section 6)

  ARDUINO — SERVOS (MG90S):
  Pan servo  (camera rotation)  : Pin 10
  Tilt servo (camera elevation) : Pin 9

  Pan horn is seated so servo 90 = camera facing front. On the wire pan is
  signed: 0 = front, +90 = left, -90 = right (PAN_CENTER_DEG / PAN_DIR in
  the firmware calibration block).

------------------------------------------------
5. DRIVE MOTORS + ENCODERS (2x JGA25-370)
------------------------------------------------
Each motor has a 6-pin JST-PH plug (2.0mm pitch).

  MOTOR POWER (motor wire --> shield):
  Right motor +  (Green)  --> M1 terminal (+)
  Right motor -  (Black)  --> M1 terminal (-)
  Left motor  +  (Green)  --> M4 terminal (+)
  Left motor  -  (Black)  --> M4 terminal (-)
  M2, M3 terminals: unused

  Firmware: motorRight = AF_DCMotor(1), motorLeft = AF_DCMotor(4).
  If a wheel spins the wrong way, flip its LEFT/RIGHT_MOTOR_DIR in the
  firmware's calibration block and reflash (don't swap the wires).

  ENCODERS (encoder wire --> Arduino):
  Right motor signal A  (Yellow) --> A0
  Right motor signal B  (White)  --> A1
  Left motor  signal A  (Yellow) --> A2
  Left motor  signal B  (White)  --> A3
  Encoder VCC, both  (Red)    --> Arduino 5V   [shared]
  Encoder GND, both  (Orange) --> Arduino GND  [shared]

  A0-A3 have no external interrupt (INT0/INT1) on the Uno. The firmware
  decodes them with pin-change interrupts (PCINT8-11, full 4x quadrature)
  and the Jetson reads the cumulative counts back over I2C: a 10-byte read
  from 0x08 = [0xE5][int32 left][int32 right][sum8], little-endian.
  Count direction: LEFT/RIGHT_ENC_DIR in the firmware calibration block.

  WIRE COLOR MAP (motor's original leads --> new connector):
  Role               Old      New
  Motor +            Red      Green
  Motor -            White    Black
  Encoder signal A   Yellow   Yellow
  Encoder signal B   Green    White
  Encoder VCC (+)    Blue     Red
  Encoder GND (-)    Black    Orange

  CAREFUL: in the new colors RED is encoder 5V, not motor power.

------------------------------------------------
6. POWER (12V battery pack)
------------------------------------------------
Battery (+) --> Switch (in)
Switch (out) splits into two branches:
  Branch 1 --> Shield EXT_PWR (+)        [straight 12V, motors]
  Branch 2 --> Buck converter IN+        [mini560 PRO, fixed 9V out]
               Buck OUT+ --> barrel plug red wire --> Arduino barrel jack

Battery (-) = common ground rail. Ties together ALL of:
  - Shield EXT_PWR (-)
  - Buck converter IN-
  - Buck converter OUT-
  - Barrel plug black wire --> Arduino barrel jack sleeve

The switch is on battery (+) only, before the split: one switch cuts
power to both the shield and the Arduino. Battery (-) is never switched.

*** REMOVE THE PWR JUMPER ON THE MOTOR SHIELD. With the jumper on,
    EXT_PWR (12V) is tied to the Arduino's VIN, which connects the
    12V and 9V branches. ***

* The 12V and 9V branches must not touch anywhere except the shared
  ground rail. A short between them puts 12V into the Arduino.
* The buck converter is fixed 9V (no trimpot), but check OUT+/OUT-
  with a multimeter once before first connecting it to the Arduino.

------------------------------------------------
7. ARDUINO UNO PIN USAGE
------------------------------------------------
D0, D1    Serial (USB debug output, 9600 baud)
D2        FREE   (the only free external-interrupt pin, INT0)
D3        Shield: M2 PWM  (channel unused)
D4        Shield: 74HC595 shift register
D5        Shield: M3 PWM  (channel unused)
D6        Shield: M4 PWM  (left motor)
D7, D8    Shield: 74HC595 shift register
D9        Tilt servo
D10       Pan servo
D11       Shield: M1 PWM  (right motor)
D12       Shield: 74HC595 shift register
D13       FREE   (onboard LED)
A0-A3     Motor encoders                       (section 5)
A4, A5    I2C SDA/SCL to Jetson, via level shifter (section 4)
5V, GND   Level shifter HV side + both encoders
Barrel    9V from buck converter               (section 6)

------------------------------------------------
WIRING NOTES:
------------------------------------------------
* THE I2S BUS: Clocks (Pin 12 and 35) are shared. Both the mic and the amp
  connect to these exact same pins on the Jetson.
* GROUND: All grounds are connected together to form a common reference.
* POWER: Power lines must remain isolated! The mic needs 3.3V, and the amp
  needs 5V to drive the speaker.
* LEVEL SHIFTER: Always connect LV side to Jetson (3.3V) and HV side to
  Arduino (5V). Swapping sides will not work and may damage the Jetson.
